Assistant — Records Search—

Searches the records saved in this app — vessels, clients, engagements, inquiries, follow-ups, appointments and drafts — and nothing else. It does not invent specifications or reach outside this file.

What it can do

Find any saved record by name, place, status or note text · jump straight to the record to edit it · count what is on the board. What it cannot do: send email (drafts only, like the rest of this app), publish listings, or fill in a specification nobody recorded — unknown specs stay “Not recorded”.

How this app is made

Palette, interpreted from the company's own site: the grand charcoal-and-gold of the Lions Gate header over an airy white page, bright gold kept for the wordmark moments, and the site's surprise cyan reserved for the numbered wayfinding — charcoal #302F2E, gold #F1D51B, cyan #1BB4E8.

Type: headings are set in Georgia — the disclosed stand-in for the site's classic serif — with rounded geometric titles for the fleet, as on the site. No webfont is claimed where Georgia is used.

One self-contained file; no external requests; works offline. Records save in this browser's local storage under the key lionsGateYachts.entries.v1 (about 5 MB assumed). Export a JSON backup from Accounts before moving machines.

Captain Training appears in this app only as an internal tracking category on the Sea-Trial & Service board — it is not a service Lions Gate offers.
