Follow One Thing — the Currents of Canopy

Canopy is not a map of places — it is a map of movements. Everything a person uses — work or personal — plugs into one app, and this page follows real things as they travel through it: a storm warning, a paycheque, a listing photo, a yacht walkthrough video, a lead, a day. Click any ribbon, any app, any area, or anything in the lists below and it roadmaps itself — its whole path lights up, with a plain explanation of where it goes and what it does there. If there is no ribbon, there is no link: nothing here is implied by two things sitting near each other.

The two app boxes, opened up

Workbench is a sandbox: a main app plugs in, and everything that app carries then works across Canopy. A service worked into one app sits under that app — never loose under Workbench — and own-account services (an accounts program, a customer system) appear under every app that uses them, as that app's own copy. Open any app to see what is inside it and what it uses.

The chains — what joining things up makes possible

Sixteen chains are switched on, and new ones get added as they surface. You set the thresholds — how much snow, how much warning — and one rule always holds: chains draft, people decide. Every message to a client stays a draft until a person approves it.

How this page stays honest. Status is only ever one of three words — Working, Optional, Watch list — and the paid services Grant ruled out are shown marked out, not hidden: the paid all-in-one media editor and Matterport are out. There are no bare numbers: every count is written in plain words (the traffic cameras are "432 live traffic camera views — 403 City of Ottawa cameras and 29 Ontario highway cameras, from the City's published list"; the City notes about 275 physical cameras are installed, because one location can carry several list entries). God's Eye View (Osiris) lives inside SITREP and is shown in this page's own style — it never gets its own box. There is no scanner feed anywhere on this page: Grant removed it.
