Canopy — what we're using

The everything page lists every option the team found; this page is the actual set in use, presented as one working system. Everything here is connected, built into Canopy, or approved by Grant and being wired in — except the add-on options, which are labelled Optional: add them if you have them. The paid services Grant ruled out are not on this page at all. Put together 22 November 2026.

How to read the label on every entry:

Connected now the connection is live today — the service is signed in and working with the team.

Built into Canopy part of Canopy itself: working in the current build, or wired by design so it lights up when Canopy's sections are packaged into one app.

Approved — wiring in Grant has said yes; the team is wiring it into the app.

Optional — add if you have it not part of the working set — an alternative or add-on a person picks at setup because it is the system they actually have.

The big idea, in Grant's words: everybody has hundreds of apps. Canopy takes everything a person uses and does — work or personal — plugs it into one app that combines and links and figures everything out, and helps them run their life with the AI team. Not just for Grant or Johnny — for his sisters, his dad, or old Betty Lou walking down the side of the road. Every list on this page is judged against that: it has to work for anybody.

1 · Home and SITREP

Home is the wall — weather, commute, markets, team, and the world at a glance. SITREP is the situation report that sits inside Home (Grant's ruling, 22 November 2026: SITREP is a section within Home, not its own tab): Weather, Commute and Cameras, Live TV, Airport, and World. Weather Mode: the weather spot adapts to whatever is actually happening — snow, rain, heat, storm — brings the right SITREP card forward, then settles back when it clears.
