Canopy — every connector and app the team found

Everything the team has worked into Canopy, or found and checked for it, in one place. Put together 22 November 2026 from the team's roadmap, its setup catalogues, and its research hunts. Nothing here was connected, bought, or built just because it appears on this page — every entry says honestly how it connects and where it stands.

How to read the label on every entry: every entry carries exactly one of three labels. How something connects — a live feed, a link-out door, a file import — is said in the entry's own words, never in the label.

Working wired in, adopted, or connected — part of Canopy now.

Optional an alternative on a pick-list inside its own area — there if it is yours, picked at setup.

Watch list a named candidate, not connected — the team found it and checked it; it connects only if Grant says so.

Paid and ruled-out items are not in the pick-lists at all — they sit together in section 11, with their reasons.

Ruled by Grant on 22 November 2026: Wire-in first — everything gets wired into the app wherever a real route exists; a link out is the exception, never the default. Free only — paid candidates are out: no new paid services. Accounts a business already holds still come in — Cityscape already uses Samsara, so Samsara comes in on that account. All candidates in — every option the team found is listed, and the pick-lists now live inside their own areas on this page, so you see the list where you pick.
The big idea, in Grant's words: everybody has hundreds of apps. Canopy takes everything a person uses and does — work or personal — plugs it into one app that combines and links and figures everything out, and helps them run their life with the AI team. Not just for Grant or Johnny — for his sisters, his dad, or old Betty Lou walking down the side of the road. Every list on this page is judged against that: it has to work for anybody.

The banking pick-list — every money route found

The honest shape: no bank offers a public connection to a personal hub. Every live route runs through a middleman (an "aggregator") or a statement file. Canadian open banking is not live yet — the Bank of Canada oversees it and draft rules are expected in June 2026 — so Canopy builds on middlemen now and designs for the framework later. Nobody should ever be told open banking is live.

  • Plaid — the middleman. It holds direct data-access agreements with RBC (2022) and TD (2023): for those banks, no login details are shared at all — the bank authorises the connection itself. No published prices: the trial is free, then pay-as-you-go or a quote. Plaid's 2022 settlement (58 million United States dollars, no admission of wrongdoing) is disclosed, not hidden — Canopy links to it.Watch list
  • Statement import — for any bank the middlemen do not cover: the person brings a statement file. There is no scraping fallback, ever.Working
  • Canadian money depth the hunt also found — Inverite · Borrowell (free credit score) · Equifax and TransUnion Canada · Plooto, Rotessa, Payment Evolution (payments) · Interac · tax pages (link-out).Optional
The safety shape belongs on the setup screen itself: read-only always — a Canopy bank connection can see balances and transactions; it cannot move money. Sign-in happens in the person's own hands, in the middleman's window or the bank's own page; Canopy never stores the password. Only balances and transactions — nothing more. And it can be cut off any time, with Canopy's own one-tap disconnect.
