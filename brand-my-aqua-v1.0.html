About Brand My Aqua

Ottawa custom-branded still and sparkling spring water in aluminum cans (355 mL), sourced from the Lanark Highlands, pH 7.7, with 360° print and QR tracking on every can. Can formats: 355 Std · 355 Sleek · 473 mL. Minimum order 550 cans per design and per variety; typical turnaround 4–6 weeks; design included.
