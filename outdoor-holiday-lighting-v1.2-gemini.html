1The site

2Trees — one card per tree

Label every tree — front-left, by the entrance, patio corner — so the crew is talking about the same tree. The estimator is built into every card: the picture and the real count update as you go. Identical twins side by side can share a card with a count.

How the count works: the audited estimator engine is built in — helix wrap math plus an 800-step crown integral, on 25 ft / 50-light strings (Big Star 5mm minis), wrap spacing by fullness (trunk/canopy inches: Light 6/18 · Classic 4/12 · Full 3/8). Footage includes the routing allowance and spares are rounded once per job — both live in the Estimate & price section. This replaces the old checklist rough count (the Bank Street calibration, ~12 sets on a medium leafy tree): counts now come from the tree's actual geometry.
